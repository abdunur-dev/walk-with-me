SMS simulator

Simulator. No real messages are sent.

Type a command as a learner with no data would. Replies and alerts appear in the phone.

09… or 07… or +251…

Commands

  • HELP: a person calls you back
  • ASK and a question: a short answer from the course
  • JOBS driver adama: up to 3 jobs
  • ALERT driver adama, then YES: job alerts
  • STOP: stop alerts

Messages

    The mock gateway only keeps the last messages in memory. Nothing leaves the server.
