What it does

Ask where the button is. Focus lands on it.

Ask "where do I finish this lesson?" The helper scrolls to Mark as complete, moves keyboard and screen reader focus there, draws a ring with a label, and says "Found it". It never clicks for you.

If the control is on another page, it says which page and gives you a link.

Example from the demo lesson

You: Where do I mark this lesson done?

Helper: Found it: Mark as complete. Press Enter to use it.

Open the lesson and ask

A helper sees where you are, not your whole screen.

Press Talk to a person. Before anything is sent, you read the place message: the page, the section, the control in focus, the last error and your last three messages. You press Send or Change.

If a helper asks to see your page, you choose: share a read-only page view with every input hidden, or say no. You can stop sharing at any time. It works on phones and slow connections because it is text, not video.

Answers in Amharic, with the English term your course uses.

Ask in Amharic or English. Answers come only from the course and show where they came from, with a Take me there link to the section. Amharic answers keep the English course term in brackets so you can match it to the lesson.

Amharic text is shown only after a native speaker has checked it. Until then the helper says the Amharic answer is not ready and answers in English.

No internet? It still answers from the course, using a copy of the questions and answers built into the helper.

One line to add it to your site.

Put this before the closing body tag of each page. It loads the helper in its own isolated box, so your styles and its styles do not clash.

<script src="https://YOUR-HOST/widget/walk-with-me.js"
        data-api="https://YOUR-HOST" data-lang="am" defer></script>

Three ways to adopt it

  1. Level 1, script tag. The line above. Works on any page, including pages opened from a file.
  2. Level 2, REST API. Every feature has an endpoint, described in an OpenAPI document.
  3. Level 3, open standard. Jobs are published as schema.org/JobPosting data at /v1/jobs?format=jsonld.

Privacy

No accounts and no names. Chat text is deleted after 7 days. A call-back number is deleted when the request ends. Alert subscriptions last until you stop them. Interview support messages are never stored.
